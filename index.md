# Yue Shen

I am **Yue (Shelly) Shen (沈悦)**, a Ph.D. student in Accounting at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://sme.cuhk.edu.cn/en/node/2908).

My interest in accounting research began during my undergraduate studies at the University of Macau, where I first encountered [Ball and Brown's (1968) seminal study](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9780203067024-3/empirical-evaluation-accounting-income-numbers-ray-ball-philip-brown) in an Accounting Theory course. Their work sparked my interest in empirical accounting research and in how accounting information shapes economic decisions.

## Education

- Master of Accounting, The University of Hong Kong
- BSc in Accounting, University of Macau

## Research Interests

- Labor Markets and Human Capital
- Corporate Social Responsibility, ESG, and Greenwashing
